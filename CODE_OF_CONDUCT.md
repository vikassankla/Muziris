# Code of Conduct

Muziris adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussion about the
work, and make the project welcoming to people of every background and level of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Until a dedicated address is
published here, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments,
commits or contributions, and may ban participants, in line with the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
